About Regenerative Assets

Regenerative Assets is a software company building tools to support companies and projects that are working on innovative projects in regenerative land management, regenerative economics, and positive social impact.

Since our team has a great deal of experience with building advanced applications, most of our software is designed to operate in a with an immutable and auditable history of actions.

Regen Asset Apps embody the exact patterns for great asset registries:

  • Integrity: Tamper-proof auditable history
  • Provenance: Every entry is digitally signed and attributable
  • Low Energy Use: Lightweight systems that don't depend on energy-intensive data centers
  • Resilient: Multiple copies of data, shared across the user's devices
